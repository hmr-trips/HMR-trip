SECTION 08 // FIELD PROTOCOLS

Operational FAQs & Policies

Clear guidelines on the 10-13 December winter trip, ₹0 interest, and student safety.

How does the free "Add Interest" process work?

When you submit your Name and WhatsApp number above, you are entering the verified student interest pool for 10-13 December with zero upfront payment.

Once our batch reaches the minimum student quorum, we message you on WhatsApp to confirm. You then pay a ₹1,000 token advance via UPI to secure your physical seat. The remaining balance of ₹3,299 is paid on departure morning (10 Dec) when boarding the bus in Hamirpur.

When will we get the complete hour-by-hour itinerary plan?
The full, granular hour-by-hour roadmap, bus boarding schedules, driver contacts, and winter clothing advisory will be dispatched 2 weeks before the trip (by late November) via the official WhatsApp squad group.
Is this expedition safe for female college students and first-timers?
Yes, rigorously so. We operate with a dedicated female trip coordinator in our crew. Room arrangements and riverside tents are strictly segregated by gender unless an intact co-ed friend squad requests their own shared setup. Campsites are private and gated with round-the-clock staff, eliminating external tourist nuisance.
What happens if Atal Tunnel / Sissu is closed due to heavy snow?
In December, high-altitude mountain passes are subject to snow conditions. If the Border Roads Organisation (BRO) temporarily restricts traffic through the Atal Tunnel North Portal into Sissu, we redirect the day's mountain excursion to Solang Valley snow viewpoints or Gulaba, ensuring you enjoy the snow safely.
